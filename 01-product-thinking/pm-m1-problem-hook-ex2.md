# Strategic Product Thinking, Module 1 Lab (Exercise 2)

## Responses
- **Chosen path, which scenario are you committing to? (StreamLine or RouteLogic, or your own):** RouteLogic
- **Strategic crisis, based on the brief, if the company does nothing for 12 months, what happens to its market position?:** If RouteLogic does nothing, it risks becoming known as a powerful but slow platform, causing enterprise customers to migrate toward simpler competitors that help frontline teams move faster.
- **Moment of misery, what is the user likely doing outside the product (Google, spreadsheets, a competitor) because your tool isn’t serving them?:** A coordinator needs to make an urgent route change and quickly inform drivers and customers. Instead of completing the task within RouteLogic, they export information to spreadsheets, search through multiple screens for delivery details, call drivers directly, and use WhatsApp or SMS to communicate updates. The software intended to centralize operations becomes so cumbersome that critical work is completed outside the platform.
- **Problem hook, summarize the business risk and user pain into one urgent sentence.:** When dispatchers spend more time managing the software than managing deliveries, RouteLogic risks losing customers to leaner competitors built for speed.
- **Value proposition, based on the proposed initiative (Spotlight or Velocity), what is the new value and why is it urgent to launch now?:** Velocity streamlines the RouteLogic experience for frontline coordinators by reducing complexity and surfacing the workflows, actions, and metrics they use most, enabling faster execution and preserving customer loyalty before operational friction drives customers elsewhere.
